# Lap Vision — Friends Beta Install & Quick-Start Guide
Lap Vision turns an Android phone on a tripod into an optical start/finish line timer for motocross, karting, cycling, and running — no transponders or stickers needed.
---
## 1. Download & Install (60 Seconds)
1. Tap the latest **`LapVision-<version>.apk`** link from the [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/bgraham11/lap-vision-android-releases/releases/latest).
2. Open the downloaded `.apk` from your browser notification or **Files / Downloads**.
3. If Android asks **"Allow from this source"**, toggle it **On** for your browser and tap **Install**.
### Samsung Galaxy (One UI 6 / 7) — "Auto Blocker" Tip
Recent Samsung phones enable **Auto Blocker** by default, which blocks all apps outside the Galaxy Store / Play Store:
1. Open Android **Settings → Security and privacy → Auto Blocker**.
2. Toggle **Auto Blocker → Off** while installing or updating Lap Vision (you can turn it back on afterward; installed apps keep working).
### Google Play Protect Prompt
Because Lap Vision is distributed directly via GitHub Releases rather than the Play Store, Google Play Protect may say *"Unfamiliar app"*:
- Tap **Scan app** (recommended — Google verifies the package on-device and completes installation), **or** tap **More details → Install anyway**.
---
## 2. 60-Second Living Room Walk-Test (Before Track Day!)
Try Lap Vision at home in 60 seconds so you know how it feels before heading to the track:
1. Open **Lap Vision → Time a session**.
2. On Step 1 (**Select Sport**), tap **Running / Walk Test** *(this sets Min Lap = `3s` and enables People on Foot)*.
3. Prop your phone against a mug or tripod aimed across a hallway or doorway.
4. Drag the **Finish Line** across the floor, verify the **Direction Arrow**, and tap **DONE**.
5. Tap **START**, then walk across the line twice (~4 seconds apart) — Lap Vision will enrol you on Crossing 1 and announce your lap time out loud on Crossing 2!
---
## 3. Trackside Tips & Sending Feedback
- **Mounting**: Use a sturdy tripod or fence mount angled slightly downward (`15°–35°`) at a clean section of track where riders pass single-file or with clear separation.
- **Adjusting Rules Anytime**: Tap the **Gear (Settings)** icon to switch Sport Presets (**Motocross**, **Karting / Car**, **MTB / Cycling**, **Running / Walk Test**), tweak **Min Lap**, or toggle **People on Foot**.
- **Fixing Split / Ghost Racers**: Tap any racer in the leaderboard to **Rename**, **Merge** two IDs together, or **Drop** a false trigger.
- **1-Tap Bug / Feedback Report**: If a lap is missed, two riders swap IDs, or anything looks off:
  - Open **About → SHARE DIAGNOSTIC REPORT (.ZIP)** (or **Settings → SHARE .ZIP** inside a session) and text/Discord the small `.zip` file!
  - It bundles your track layout, timing table, engine log, and 112px rider thumbnails (**never** your full video, and nothing is ever uploaded automatically).
